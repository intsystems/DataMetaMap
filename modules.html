Top-level modules

Abstract base class BaseEmbedder and the SupportsGetItem protocol.

WassersteinEmbedder — Optimal Transport-based embedder using Bures-Wasserstein or exact EMD distances.

Dataset loaders and helpers for CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, MNIST, STL-10, iNat, and more.

data_meta_map.models

Backbone model definitions used as probe networks.

Task2Vec implementation: Task2Vec, ProbeNetwork, Embedding, similarity utilities, and variational inference tools.
